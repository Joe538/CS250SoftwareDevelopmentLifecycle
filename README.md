# CS250SoftwareDevelopmentLifecycle
This repository is an example of the things I learned throughout this course.


How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?
When reading through reading material for this course, we talked about how Amazon placed a large focus on customer feedback which revolutionized how companies grow and improve their products. So, the customer/user is a big driver in potential direction and improvement. I look at the customer needs as the essential needs for the application. The user is the reason that the program is being used thus its important to implement their needs. It's important to note that not all needs can be implemented, but when possible, it is a great way to make improvements. User stories are very helpful because they create the expectation of what should be implemented. It's crucial not to leave important details up for interpretation. 

How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?
There are many ways to approach developing programs and its important to pick a solution that works for the project and team. Two Agile processes that really stuck out to be me were Scrum and Extreme Programming. I would like to incorporate these two process in the future one day. Scrum is a great way to build and develop programs, and I think that Extreme Programming also has its strengths at times.

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?
Good software development team member have to be great team players along with great individual characteristics. Great team players are respectful, kind, communicate well, and trust their teammates. Great individuals in software development have to be resilient, motivated, critical thinkers, and committed. These qualities all make up what a good team member.
